I'm Yerramsetti Sai Mohith — a developer who finds joy in building things that are fast, smart, and purposeful. Currently a Software Engineer at Chella Software, working across performance-critical backend systems and applied ML.

I hold a B.Tech in CSE from IIITDM Kancheepuram and a B.Sc in Data Science from IIT Madras — a dual-degree path that gave me engineering rigour and analytical depth in equal measure.

On weekends I fine-tune NLP models, build computer-vision pipelines, and explore the next AI integration rabbit hole. I'm also building Decoding Life — a podcast coming soon.

"I don't just consume technology — I pull it apart, rebuild it, and ship something new every weekend."
